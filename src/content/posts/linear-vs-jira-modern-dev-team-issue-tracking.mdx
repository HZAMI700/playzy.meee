For over a decade, Atlassian Jira was the undisputed standard for software project management. However, as dev teams grew fatigued by slow page loads, complex configuration menus, and clunky ticket administration, **Linear** emerged as a lightweight, lightning-fast alternative designed specifically for high-velocity software teams.

We evaluated both issue tracking platforms across a 50-engineer organization undergoing product sprints, bug triaging, and quarterly roadmap planning.

<Callout type="tip" title="Performance Metric">
Linear pages load in under 100ms via real-time client-side sync. Jira Cloud page loads routinely range from 1.5s to 3.8s depending on installed add-ons.
</Callout>

## When Jira is Still Required

While developers prefer Linear, enterprise operations teams sometimes require Jira for legacy compliance:

- **Complex Workflow State Machines**: If your organization requires 15 mandatory sign-off stages before a ticket moves to production, Jira enforces these constraints out-of-the-box.
- **Atlassian Ecosystem Integration**: Teams heavily reliant on Confluence, Bitbucket, and Jira Service Management gain unified identity and billing.

## Verdict: The Clear Choice for Modern Dev Teams

For startup engineering teams and modern scale-ups seeking developer velocity, **Linear** is the superior product. Switching to Linear routinely saves developers 2-3 hours per week in ticket administration.
