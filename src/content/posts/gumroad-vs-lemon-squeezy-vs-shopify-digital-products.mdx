Selling digital products—such as developer boilerplates, design kits, technical ebooks, and video courses—is one of the most scalable online revenue streams. However, choosing the wrong e-commerce platform can result in massive tax compliance headaches or losing 15%+ of your revenue to hidden transaction fees.

We analyzed the three leading platforms: **Gumroad**, **Lemon Squeezy**, and **Shopify**.

<Callout type="tip" title="Global Tax Compliance Warning">
If you sell digital products to customers in the EU, UK, or US without collecting VAT/sales tax, you face severe back-tax penalties. Using a Merchant of Record (MoR) solves this automatically.
</Callout>

## Platform Breakdown

### 1. Lemon Squeezy: The Modern Gold Standard
Acquired by Stripe, Lemon Squeezy provides a sleek checkout UI, built-in license key generation for software, subscription billing support, and full MoR tax compliance.

### 2. Gumroad: Simple but Costly
Gumroad pioneered frictionless digital sales, but its decision to institute a flat 10% platform fee across all creator revenue tiers made it uncompetitive for creators generating over $1,000/month.

### 3. Shopify: Enterprise Power with Manual Tax Overhead
Shopify offers unlimited customization, custom domains, and zero platform take-rate beyond standard credit card processing. However, because Shopify is not an MoR, creators must manually register and file taxes in every jurisdiction where customers reside.

## Verdict: Which Platform Should You Choose?

- **Choose Lemon Squeezy** if you sell digital downloads, dev boilerplates, software licenses, or micro-SaaS subscriptions and want global tax peace of mind.
- **Choose Shopify** if you manage a multi-product store with physical inventory and an internal accounting team.
- **Avoid Gumroad** for high-volume sales due to their high 10% fee tax.
