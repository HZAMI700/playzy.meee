## 2. Spatial Window Arrangement Strategies

To maximize focus during deep work sessions:
1. Position your primary Mac Virtual Display centered in front of your desk.
2. Place Slack / Communication windows floating to your left side.
3. Position your Notion PKM and documentation windows to your right side.
