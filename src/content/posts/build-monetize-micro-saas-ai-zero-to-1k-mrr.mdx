## The 4-Step Launch Blueprint

### Step 1: Identify "Pain-Point" Micro Problems
Avoid building general-purpose AI chat wrappers. Focus instead on narrow, high-utility business problems:
- Automated PDF invoice extractors for freelance accountants.
- Dynamic programmatic OG thumbnail generators for Next.js blogs.
- Micro-SEO audit tools for Shopify store owners.

### Step 2: Rapid Scaffolding in 48 Hours
Utilize pre-configured Next.js boilerplates with pre-built authentication, database schema, and payment webhooks. Leverage **Cursor Composer** to implement core business logic without wasting time on custom authentication wrappers.

### Step 3: Zero-Cost Initial Customer Acquisition
1. **Show Your Work on X / LinkedIn**: Document the build process publicly using `#buildinpublic`.
2. **Product Hunt & BetaList Launch**: Schedule launch days with clear demo videos and limited-time founder discounts.
3. **SEO-Optimized Programmatic Content**: Create target comparison guides (e.g., *Alternative to X*) to capture high-intent search traffic.
